# Paid Instant Search: keep the donors or finish the fusion?

## Result

A finished fusion is useful here, but it does **not** establish a new universal counter. With equal $29 target-only material value and no overload, Instant Search draws the unfused search/suggestion branch at HP300 and HP440, and loses to six ordinary supported links. Keeping the actual acquired bridge inventory changes the comparison: the fusion then beats the ordinary alternative at HP300/440. At the genuinely purchased CPU12 capacity, it beats that alternative at all three canonical HP settings tested.

Those advantages are sensitive to startup timing. A hypothetical 0.75-second delay to the fused page changes its CPU12/HP220 win over ordinary links into a loss, and its CPU12/HP300 win over held donors into a loss. The engine's normal startup remains unchanged. This is a conditional decision witness, not broad counter diversity, a recommended blanket fusion policy, or a reason to tune combat.

## Real acquisition and exact donor branch

`scripts/paid-instant-choice-benchmark.ts` pursues two fixed production targets through the unchanged `simulateCompositionPath` offer, purchase, paid reroll, reward, battle and fusion policy. These are the **legacy eight-round expedition**, not the canonical 15-victory/eight-stage story. Nothing changes that story, combat-v4, historical replays, prices, recipes, shops, or presets.

Seed130 is selected because all seven fusion-target ingredients are genuinely owned **before round5**:

- `p8`, `go_search`: purchased for $6 in round4
- `p9`, `go_suggest`: selected from the real round4 loot offer
- Four links and one font are also owned; two navigation controls and a heading remain as paid bridge UI
- Cumulative spend at this decision is **$29 parts + $11 rerolls + $0 server = $40**. Whole-inventory input value is also $40, but that numerical equality is incidental: the font and suggestion panel were rewards, while rerolls cost money

The original donor board fights with `p8` and `p9` legally adjacent, wins round5 in 11.75 seconds, and is actually settled. Cash goes from $6 to $16. The benchmark branches that state before the fusion decision:

1. **Hold:** keep the original `p8` and `p9`
2. **Merge:** public `R.fuse` consumes exactly `p8` and `p9` and creates `p11`, `go_instant`, matching the paid path's recorded output ID

Both branches claim the same actually offered CAPTCHA administrator and keep CDN, $16 cash, CPU12 capacity, and every other owned item and property. Public editor moves arrange both choices; there is no bought or injected fused item, no donor invented afterward, no selling, free capacity, or discarded bridge. The next original fusion-target path snapshot runs three unnecessary-for-completion rerolls first, so its recorded round6 exact completion is $43 spent; the matched decision branches stop before those additional rerolls. They are not separately settled alternative campaigns.

The distinct ordinary policy uses six links, font and suggestion panel. Seed130 first completes it in round7 after **$29 parts + $17 rerolls + $0 server = $46**. Its full inventory is also $40 and contains the same two navigation controls and heading; its cash is $20 and its administrators are also CDN/CAPTCHA. This is an equal-input-value alternative, **not equal cumulative spend, round, cash, or CPU**.

## Finished comparisons and limits

Every result is replayed in both physical seats with the logical subject preserved. HP220/300/440 are shared base HP, not claims of when the board is affordable. There are three clearly different resource slices:

- **Core:** the $11 bridge remains owned but held; CPU17 and no administrators on both sides
- **Whole:** every real item is placed; CPU17 and no administrators on both sides
- **Purchased:** full inventory, each branch's actually purchased CPU12 and actually obtained CDN/CAPTCHA. This preserves overload rather than granting the normalized CPU17

The fixed earlier $23 supported-four-link counter is also present as a reference. It has CPU7, CPU17 capacity and no administrators; it is **not** an equal-$40 full-inventory opponent. All three choices beat that reference at canonical clocks across these slices, so defeating it does not uniquely justify fusion.

The fusion's two-CPU saving matters under overload: load15/12 has 1.15× lag; load13/12 has 1.05× lag. The ordinary full board has load14/12, or 1.10× lag. It also has eight navigation attackers because both paid bridge controls remain placed, invoking the existing 1.10× navigation interval factor. Hold/merge have six navigation attackers and avoid that factor. The core-only ordinary board has six links and avoids it too. Neither effect should be concealed by comparing just catalogue costs.

The fusion changes attack cadence, shared suggestion strength, and occupied area as well as CPU. These observations do **not** isolate one stat as the sole cause of every reversal. The support-connected unfused layout is a real control rather than the acquisition harness's incidental pre-fusion layout; every non-donor part has the exact same geometry in hold and merge.

### Startup stress, distinct from production rules

The engine normally gives components their existing half-period startup (including any faction rule). The stress test adds 0.75 seconds to all timed components on either the logical subject or the logical opponent, separately; it never changes periods, damage, later clocks or gameplay. The report records canonical and perturbed startup times. These are hypothetical robustness checks, not reachable editor options or canonical replay claims.

Examples of failures of robustness:

- Whole CPU17/HP300: delaying the fused page changes its 65HP win over the ordinary alternative into a loss with 57.6 enemy HP
- Purchased CPU12/HP220: delaying the fused page changes its 10.7HP win over the ordinary alternative into a loss with 1.288 enemy HP
- Purchased CPU12/HP300: delaying the fused page changes its 43.824HP win over held donors into a loss with 4.64 enemy HP

Both seats agree on winner, elapsed time and logical normalized HP margin in every measured case. No production balance change is proposed from this bounded evidence. Browser appearance remains outside this check.
